# Start 'Em, Sit 'Em: The Week 15 Movers

Every fantasy column in America this week will tell you to start Ja'Marr
Chase. Groundbreaking stuff. You did not need me, a model, or a Mac Mini
named Earnest to arrive at "play your studs."

So this column does the opposite. Every name below is a MOVER -- a guy
whose chance of a startable week jumped or slid the most against his own
recent form. Not the top of the board. The part of the board where you're
actually chewing your fingernails Sunday morning.

## Buy: Four Guys Trending Up (One With a Catch)

**Woody Marks, RB, Houston (vs ARI) -- 48%, up from 40%.** Marks has crept
from a touch-share afterthought to a projected 19-carry back, and this week
Arizona is the kind of front that stops arguing with you. Just under a
coin flip to give you a startable line, which for a guy you probably
streamed off waivers is a genuine RB2 you can feel okay about.

**Puka Nacua, WR, Rams (vs DET) -- 48%, up from 37%.** The volume came back.
Nacua's projected targets ticked up and the Detroit secondary is a friendlier
room than what he'd been walking into. He'd quietly dipped into flex-anxiety
territory the last couple weeks; the model just walked him back toward the
WR2 he's supposed to be. Roughly a startable week every other time out, and
trending the right way.

**Harold Fannin Jr., TE, Cleveland (vs CHI) -- 53%, up from 42%.** Here's
the one I'd actually circle. Tight end is a swamp -- half the position is a
coin flip to see three targets -- and Fannin has pulled himself to a
better-than-even shot at a usable week on nothing but earned role. No injury
in front of him, no fluky touchdown propping it up. Just a rookie the offense
keeps feeding. If you've been streaming the position and losing, this is the
guy to stop streaming.

**Kyle Pitts, TE, Atlanta (vs TB) -- 56%, up from 43% -- but read this
part.** On talent, Pitts is the best riser on the board: a projected uptick
in targets pushes him to a better-than-even week, the kind of Pitts line
we've been waiting three years for. The catch is the (Q) next to his name.
Here's how this board treats that: the Tuesday number assumes he plays,
because on Tuesday nobody is officially anything. As the practice reports
land Thursday and Saturday, the number re-scores -- and if he lands on the
inactive list, the story was never the 56%, it was the status. Draft him
into your lineup, absolutely. Then check back before kickoff, because this
is exactly the pick the model reserves the right to change its mind on.

## Sell: Three Guys the Model Soured On

**Rashee Rice, WR, Kansas City (vs LAC) -- 27%, down from 40%.** This one's
all matchup. The Chargers are the toughest cover Rice has drawn in a month,
and the model does not care how his name sounds -- it dropped him to about a
one-in-four shot at a startable line. Not a bench-him-forever call. A
this-week, this-defense call.

**Brock Bowers, TE, Las Vegas (vs PHI) -- 41%, down from 58%.** The biggest
faller on the entire board, and it stings because Bowers has been close to a
weekly lock. Philadelphia is a brutal draw, his projected targets slipped,
and the sure thing quietly became a coin flip. You're still probably playing
him -- there's nobody better at the position on your bench -- but go in with
your eyes open, not your usual shrug.

**Kyren Williams, RB, Rams (vs DET) -- 34%, down from 41%.** A quieter slide,
but a real one: the projected workload came down a couple of touches and that's
enough to knock a fringe RB2 down to one-in-three. If you've got a healthy
alternative, this is the week to look at it.

## One More, Because It's Fun

The model also soured on **Justin Jefferson** (44% to 31%) -- and here's the
tell that these numbers aren't just reading the matchup off a napkin. Dallas
is actually an *easier* draw than what Jefferson's been facing. The model
faded him anyway, purely on a projected dip in targets. Nobody on Earth is
benching Justin Jefferson. But if he lays an egg this week, remember that the
number saw it coming, and it wasn't the defense that gave it away.
